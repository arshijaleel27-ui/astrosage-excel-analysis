# Astrosage Data Analysis (Excel)

## Project Overview
A comprehensive data analysis on the Astrosage dataset to uncover key insights, trends, and patterns.

## Deliverables Included
* `astrosage_analysis.xlsx`: Complete workbook with formulas, pivot tables, and data cleaning steps.
* `astrosage_report.docx`: Detailed project write-up, methodology, and recommendations.
* `astrosage_presentation.pptx`: Executive slide deck summarizing major takeaways.

## Key Insights & Executive Summary

* **Revenue Driver by Channel:** Direct **Calls** serve as the primary revenue stream, generating significantly higher revenue (~₹1.70L) compared to **Chat** consultations (~₹45k), while Complementary and Public Live Calls contribute minimally.
* **Call Completion Rate:** Only **41%** of total calls were successfully completed, while **21%** went unanswered, **15%** were busy, and **12%** failed—highlighting a major operational opportunity to reduce dropped calls and improve completion rates.
* **Platform Distribution & Scale:** The **App** dominates as the primary channel for overall calls compared to web/dashboard routes, driving a high volume of **8,508 total calls** across **133 astrologers** and generating **₹2,14,066** in total revenue.
